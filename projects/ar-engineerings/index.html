01 / The project

Engineering, clearly communicated.

Designed, developed, and deployed on-site in Saudi Arabia, the AR Engineerings website delivers a professional digital presence for an industrial and construction services company. The platform combines cinematic industrial visuals, bilingual content, carefully structured service pages, and modern interactions to communicate technical expertise and corporate credibility.

02 / The approach

Design with purpose.
Built with care.

  1. 01

    Give expertise a clear structure.

    A cinematic industrial hero introduces the company’s capabilities. Structured service pages, leadership information, and client sections help visitors explore the organisation and find the right point of contact.

  2. 02

    Build for two languages and every screen.

    English and Arabic localisation includes right-to-left layouts and responsive navigation. The delivery includes performance optimisation, technical SEO, structured metadata, Cloudflare integration, and production deployment.
