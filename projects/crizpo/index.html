01 / The project

Food, brought to life.

Designed, developed, and launched on-site in Saudi Arabia, CRIZPO is a modern restaurant website built around immersive food storytelling. The experience combines engaging product visuals, animated sections, responsive layouts, and accessible navigation to create a memorable digital experience for customers.

02 / The approach

Design with purpose.
Built with care.

  1. 01

    Let the food lead.

    Bold typography and expressive food imagery establish the restaurant’s identity. Animated sections guide the journey from the first impression to the menu, with clear routes to discover dishes and get in touch.

  2. 02

    Make every screen feel considered.

    English and Arabic content, responsive layouts, and touch-friendly navigation bring the experience to smaller screens. WhatsApp integration gives customers a direct way to contact the restaurant.
